# Shipping and Delivery Policy

Policy ID: SF-SHIPPING-001
Version: 1.0
Category: Shipping
Store: SupportFlow Demo Store

## Order Tracking
Customers may request tracking updates for their own orders.

## Delivery Support
1. Authenticate the customer.
2. Retrieve the order and shipment status.
3. Share only verified tracking information.
4. Explain any confirmed delivery delays.
5. Escalate unknown or conflicting shipment information.

## Delayed Deliveries
When a delivery is delayed, support must check the latest
available shipment status before advising the customer.

## Missing Deliveries
Reports of missing packages require investigation and
human review before any resolution is promised.

## Restrictions
The AI must not invent tracking numbers, delivery dates,
courier updates or compensation offers.

## Reference Practices
Inspired by Amazon, Alibaba.com and eBay delivery support.
Original fictional-store policy.
