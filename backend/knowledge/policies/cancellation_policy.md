# Order Cancellation Policy

Policy ID: SF-CANCEL-001
Version: 1.0
Category: Cancellation
Store: SupportFlow Demo Store

## Cancellation Eligibility
Orders may be cancelled only before shipment.

## Cancellation Process
1. Authenticate the customer.
2. Verify ownership of the order.
3. Retrieve the current shipment status.
4. If not shipped, submit a cancellation request.
5. Wait for authorized human approval.
6. Communicate only the verified final decision.

## Shipped Orders
Orders already shipped cannot be cancelled under this policy.
The customer may be directed to the applicable returns process.

## Human Approval
Cancellation execution always requires human approval.
The AI must never cancel an order independently or claim
that a cancellation has been completed.
