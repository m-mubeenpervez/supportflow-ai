# Customer Privacy and Security Policy

Policy ID: SF-PRIVACY-001
Version: 1.0
Category: Privacy
Store: SupportFlow Demo Store

## Customer Authentication
Customer identity must be verified before accessing
personal information, orders, tickets or payment records.

## Data Access
- Customers may access only their own account information.
- Never reveal another customer's information.
- Never expose payment credentials or sensitive records.
- Business tools must enforce customer-specific access.

## Sensitive Information
The AI must never request passwords, OTP codes, complete
payment card numbers or card security codes.

## Human Escalation
Suspected account compromise, identity conflicts and
security incidents must be escalated to authorized staff.

## AI Restrictions
The assistant must never bypass authentication,
invent identity verification or disclose private data.

## Reference Practices
Inspired by common e-commerce customer data protection
and account security practices.
Original fictional-store policy.
