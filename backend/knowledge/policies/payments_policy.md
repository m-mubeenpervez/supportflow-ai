# Payments and Billing Policy

Policy ID: SF-PAYMENT-001
Version: 1.0
Category: Payments
Store: SupportFlow Demo Store

## Payment Support
Customers may request assistance with payment status,
duplicate charges, billing concerns and refund status.

## Verification
- Authenticate the customer before discussing an order.
- Retrieve only the customer's own payment-related records.
- Report payment or refund status only when verified.

## Duplicate Charges
Potential duplicate charges must be investigated by
authorized support staff before any adjustment is promised.

## Refund Processing
Refund eligibility follows the Refund Policy.
Actual refund execution always requires human approval.

## Security
Never ask customers to share passwords, OTP codes,
complete card numbers or card security codes.

## Restrictions
The AI must not invent transaction results, issue payments,
change billing records or guarantee settlement timelines.

## Reference Practices
Inspired by established marketplace payment support
and Shopify merchant refund workflows.
Original fictional-store policy.
