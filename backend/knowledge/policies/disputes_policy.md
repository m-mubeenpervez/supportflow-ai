# Buyer Disputes and Complaints Policy

Policy ID: SF-DISPUTE-001
Version: 1.0
Category: Disputes
Store: SupportFlow Demo Store

## Supported Complaints
- Damaged or incorrect products
- Delayed or missing deliveries
- Refund-related complaints
- Order cancellation disputes
- Payment or billing concerns

## Complaint Handling
1. Authenticate the customer.
2. Record the reported issue.
3. Verify relevant order information.
4. Request necessary supporting evidence.
5. Review applicable company policies.
6. Escalate unresolved or disputed cases to human support.

## Human Review
Disputed eligibility, conflicting evidence and sensitive
financial decisions require authorized human review.

## Restrictions
The AI must not guarantee refunds, compensation,
cancellations or dispute outcomes.

Inspired by Alibaba.com Trade Assurance and eBay
