# Refund Policy

Policy ID: SF-REFUND-001
Version: 1.0
Category: Refunds
Store: SupportFlow Demo Store
Status: Demo policy for AI support training

## Overview
SupportFlow Demo Store reviews refund requests for
damaged or incorrect products.

## Refund Eligibility
- Customers may request a refund within 14 days of delivery.
- The product must be damaged or incorrect.
- Customers should provide their order ID and issue details.
- Supporting evidence, such as product photographs,
  may be requested.
- Eligibility is subject to order and evidence review.

## Refund Request Process
1. Verify the customer's identity.
2. Retrieve the customer's order information.
3. Confirm the delivery date and reported problem.
4. Check whether the request meets the 14-day policy.
5. Collect supporting evidence when necessary.
6. Submit an eligible request for human review.

## Human Approval
All refund execution requires authorized human approval.

The AI assistant must never:
- Process a refund independently.
- Guarantee approval before review.
- Claim money was returned before confirmation.
- Invent refund amounts or payment timelines.

## Refund Status
Customers may request updates on their refund cases.
The assistant must report only verified case information.

## Exceptions and Escalation
Unclear eligibility, missing delivery details, payment
disputes, or requests outside the policy window must
be escalated for human review.

## Customer Privacy
Only authenticated customers may access their own
order and refund information.

## Reference Practices
Inspired by public customer-support practices of:
- Amazon: returns and refunds
- Alibaba.com: Trade Assurance and buyer protection
- Shopify: refund processing

This is an original fictional-store policy.
It is not an official policy of those platforms.
